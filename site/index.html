Trunk diameter (DBH) is measured at breast height in centimetres. Native status is resolved per species against a curated list of Ontario-native trees; a tree the City recorded only to genus stays “not identified to species” rather than being guessed at. Positions come from the City's inventory, which geocodes each tree to its street address — so dots sit on the parcel, not on the exact trunk.
