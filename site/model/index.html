How good is it?

Tested on City street trees in neighbourhoods it never trained on, the model names the right genus 76% of the time and the right species 76% of the time (for the 28 species it knows). It does worse on trees unlike its training data. Against the Annex Neighbourwoods volunteer survey it got 47% of City-owned trees and 26% of private trees right. Northwest Toronto was photographed before the leaves were out, and accuracy there drops to 63%. Ravine and park woodland looks nothing like the street trees it learned from, and it calls much of it ash; treat guesses there with suspicion.

Tree locations are the City’s topographic tree points (2021), plus street trees from the inventory that have no topographic point nearby. The model only knows 23 genera; anything else is “other”.
