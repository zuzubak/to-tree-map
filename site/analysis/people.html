Distribution

How this is measured

Population comes from the 2021 Census at the dissemination area level — 3,727 of them inside Toronto, averaging about 750 residents each. Every tree is assigned to the area its coordinates fall in.

Areas rather than individual census blocks, for two reasons: block-level population is only published in a file StatCan's firewall will not serve to a script, and a block holding four trees produces a ratio that swings wildly on one record.

What this cannot tell you

A high ratio can mean nobody lives there. The extreme is . That is an arithmetically correct way of saying it is a ravine. Quintile classes keep one such area from taking over the colour scale, but read the top class with that in mind.

Eight areas have no recorded residents at all and are left grey rather than given an infinite ratio.

Census boundaries are not catchments. A resident on the edge of an area walks under the next area's trees, and this measure cannot see that.
